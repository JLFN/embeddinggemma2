## 1. What is being compressed

Source: google/embeddinggemma-2 at revision 914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b,
model.safetensors, 1488.74 MB of bf16. 1376 tensors, classified by eg2_pack's
classify() from the checkpoint's own module layout (docs/tensor-inventory.tsv).
Six classes carry VQ payloads: text-tower, text-ple, audio-attn, audio-ffn,
audio-frontend (the lconv module's linears) and the vision tower's attention and FFN.
The 262144x512 token table is a block-scalar slot, and 914 small tensors (norms,
biases, per-channel scales, conv kernels, position tables, the four projection heads)
ship verbatim at source precision.

## 3. The tier decision

The artifact ships dim 4 with a 4096-entry codebook (vq4x4096) for every VQ class, and
the token table ships at q8_0.

Why dim 4 rather than dim 8: at the same 12-bit index width, dim 4 measured 0.9869 on
the text tower against 0.9218 for dim 8 (8x4096), and 0.9858 against 0.9144 on the
vision FFN. Fewer weights per codebook entry is simply finer granularity. Dim 8 buys
size (141.9 MB of VQ payload against 237.3 MB) and costs about 0.07 cosine; it is the
right choice if the artifact must be smaller, not if quality leads.

Why 4096 and not 1024 or 2048: the DeepSeek V4.1 recipe's measured bar, recorded in
RECIPE-deepseek41-vq.md, is a routed-expert reconstruction cosine of 0.925 at 13 bits
and 0.910-0.911 at 12 bits, with 17.06 percent relative error at layer 20. At 4/1024
the text tower measures 0.9737 and the text PLE 0.9728 with 22.8 and 23.2 percent
relative error — above the bar's cosine but well above its relative error. At 4/2048
they are 0.9814 and 0.9804. At 4/4096 the text tower reaches 0.9869 and 16.16 percent
relative error, the PLE 0.9857 and 16.86 percent, and the easy classes (audio FFN and
attention) 0.9993 and 0.9994. 4/4096 is the only tier measured here whose relative
error is at the DeepSeek recipe's level, and it is the highest quality measured at all.

Why q8_0 for the token table: q8_K is not a weight format in this toolchain. Its trait
entry is can_quantize=false (quants_p1.inc.c:55), ds4q_quantize_chunk has no q8_K
branch, there is no ds4_deq_q8_K in the reader, and both engines use Q8_K only for
activation blocks (core_types.h:16, ds4.c:1322,4581). q8_0 is the 8-bit weight type
that is supported end to end: encoder at quants_p3.inc.c:276, gold decoder
ds4_quantfmt.h:45, 34 bytes per 32 values (ds4_quantfmt.c:17). The token table goes
from 268.44 MB to 142.61 MB, measured round trip cosine 1.0000 and 0.5484 percent
relative error.

## 6. What the encode costs (measured)

Per matrix, at 4/4096 on this machine's CPU (single process, i7-14700F): 12.8 s for a
0.59 M-element vision attention matrix, 19.4 s for a 1.05 M-element audio attention
matrix, 23.3 s for a 2.36 M-element vision FFN, 27.9 s for a 4.19 M-element audio FFN,
19.6 s for a 1.05 M-element text-tower matrix and 34.2 s for the 6.29 M-element text
PLE. At 4/1024 the same matrices cost 3.5 to 8.6 s, so the codebook size is the cost
driver, not the element count. The whole plan is about two hours single-process; it is
run as 14 shards and merged (see eg2_pack's merge mode, proved byte-identical to a
single-process run).

Why the GPU is not used for this tier: vq_qc.h only takes its GPU paths when
vqg_shm_ok(nc,dim) passes, and that gate measures the codebook against
cudaDevAttrMaxSharedMemoryPerBlock, which is 49152 bytes on this card. Dim 4 with 4096
entries needs 81920 bytes and dim 8 with 4096 needs 147456, so at this tier the
reference's kernels cannot launch at all and the encoder runs on the CPU by the
reference's own rule. The reference's device-resident encoder for exactly this shape
(dim 8 / nc 4096, codebook chunked into shared memory) is gguf-tools/quantize/v41_vq.cu;
adopting it is the GPU route for a later unit, and it writes its own payload layout.

## 7. Corrections and superseded numbers

- An earlier draft of this file estimated 283.1 MB total and 5.26x on the strength of an
  unmeasured tier choice. Superseded by the plan report above: 421.60 MB, 3.53x.
- An earlier draft reported "all-hot 345.2 MB, 4.31x". Superseded: that was the 4/512
  configuration, whose measured artifact total is 353.1 MB.
- The class classifier had a substring bug: "conv_projection" contains "v_proj", so the
  audio subsample-conv projection weights were classified audio-attn. Fixed in
  eg2_pack's classify(), which now catches ".subsample_conv_projection." and
  "patch_embedder." before the prefix rules; the affected tensors are verbatim slots in
  the keep class and the 24 audio-frontend matrices are the lconv module's linears.

## 8. What this page is not

It is not a measurement of the artifact: the artifact's own numbers (per tensor, by
class, with the container's checks) are in out/encode-log.txt and out/verify-log.txt.
It is not a measurement of a running engine: no engine can load this container yet.
ds4-dfm-rs has no embedding_gemma family support (no gemma string in its crates or
ds4.c, no embeddings endpoint), which is why the artifact is a preparation for a family
port rather than something to run today.
